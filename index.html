Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

MainStreet Local (MSL) is an early-stage project building a unified information layer for the physical local economy. We are developing technology to make the specific products and services on local store shelves searchable and discoverable online.

The "Invisible Shelf" refers to local dark-data: valuable products that exist in the real world but have no digital representation. If a store has an item in stock but you can't find that fact through a search engine, that product is sitting on an invisible shelf.

We aren't a map or a review site. Google and Yelp tell you *where* a business is; MSL is building the technology to tell you *what* is inside that business. We want to enhance search results by providing the missing inventory data layer.

Our core mission is to empower independent local merchants. We are designing the system to be accessible to local businesses without requiring them to manage complicated new software or pay prohibitive fees.

MSL is currently in active development. This site is the beginning of the system, focused on collecting community feedback and founding support while we test our data collection and unification prototypes.

You can help by submitting feedback through our form, sharing the mission with your neighbors, or becoming a Founding Supporter to help fund the research and development required to launch the network.

Information provided to MSL is used specifically to help us understand local economic needs and build a more useful discovery system. We value the privacy of our community and will never sell your personal data to third parties.
